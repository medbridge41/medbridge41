Questions

Frequently asked questions

Is MBBS/MD in Georgia taught in English?

Some universities offer English-language Medical Doctor programs. Confirm the exact program and language of instruction with the university.

What is the total cost?

Costs vary by university, accommodation, exchange rates and personal expenses. MedBridge currently advertises an approximate overall budget of ₹30–35 lakh for selected options; obtain a current written fee breakdown before making a decision.

What about NMC eligibility for Indian students?

Eligibility and licensing rules are set by the relevant authorities and can change. Students should check the latest NMC requirements and the exact university/program status before enrollment.

Does MedBridge help with the visa process?

MedBridge can provide guidance on documentation and the application process. Visa decisions are made by the relevant authorities.
